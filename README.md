# Word Quest

Play the game: https://tide-recording-continue-roller.trycloudflare.com

That page is the app. This file is only a short note. You can also open `index.html` in your browser.

A correct answer throws sparkles. Ten new words at a time, harder as you go, a quiz every 30 words, and you can keep playing with no time limit. When the word list ends, it starts again and your sparkies stay. Progress stays in the browser.
